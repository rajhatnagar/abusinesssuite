Get to know

How does the credit system work?

Credits are used only when the AI processes something for you — for example generating content, summarising files, or analysing data. Each action has a fixed credit cost so you always know what you’re spending. Unused credits stay in your account and reset only at the start of the next billing cycle, depending on your plan.

Can I cancel my subscription any time?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time directly from your account settings. Your plan will stay active until the end of the current billing period, and you won’t be charged again after that. There are no cancellation fees or long-term contracts.

Which social platforms are supported?

The platform currently supports the main channels most small teams and creators actually use — including LinkedIn, Instagram, X (Twitter), and Facebook. We’re adding support for additional platforms based on real user demand rather than trying to support everything at once.

Is there a limit on file size for uploads?

Yes, there is a practical limit to keep processing fast and reliable. Most common documents such as PDFs, spreadsheets, and text files work without issues. If a file is too large, the system will let you know before processing so you can split it into smaller parts.

Does the AI support languages other than English?

Yes. The AI works well with most major languages and can generate or analyse content in the language you choose. English usually delivers the most consistent results, but for everyday workflows like summaries, documents, and social content, other languages are supported without needing extra setup.
