How is your AI training different from online courses or self-paced platforms?
Our programs are built around your team, not a generic curriculum. Every session is live, hands-on, and tailored to the tools and workflows your organization actually uses. You get real instructor feedback, peer learning, and exercises designed around your industry — not pre-recorded videos your team will never finish.
Do employees need a technical background to participate?
Not at all. Our programs are designed for every role — from executives and marketers to ops leads and finance teams. We adjust the depth, language, and exercises based on who’s in the room. No coding, no data science, no prior AI experience required.
Can you customize training for our specific industry or tools?
Yes — that’s the default, not the exception. We build every program around your team’s actual workflows, the AI tools you already use (Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, or your own stack), and the compliance or regulatory context your industry requires.
How long does a typical training program take?
It depends on your goals and team size. Programs range from a focused half-day workshop to an 8-week multi-track rollout. We scope the right format with you upfront — so you’re not paying for more than you need, and your team isn’t pulled away from work longer than necessary.
How do you measure whether the training actually worked?
We use pre- and post-training assessments to track skill progression, plus 30-day adoption check-ins to measure real behavioral change. Enterprise clients receive dedicated progress tracking and a summary report showing confidence gains, tool adoption rates, and ROI indicators.
What kind of support does your team provide during and after training?
Every program includes live instructor support throughout the sessions, plus post-training resources your team can reference on the job. Enterprise clients get dedicated account management, ongoing reinforcement materials, and access to follow-up sessions to ensure adoption sticks beyond day one.
